## Original full assertion

`ExponentialTailConjecture` asserts that there exist positive real c₁,c₂ such that for every integer n≥1, every deterministic real matrix center with Euclidean operator norm at most one, every real 0<σ≤1 and every real x≥1, the actual probability of GEPP growth exceeding x(n/σ)^c₁ is at most 2^(−c₂x). Real powers, actual Gaussian probability and the complete unrestricted x range are retained. The target is its full negation, not a statement about selected exponents, fixed dimensions, zero center, small noise, finite samples or a scalar surrogate.

The Euclidean operator norm is explicitly `Matrix.toEuclideanCLM`'s norm; no default matrix norm is used for the center. Gaussian noise has the full nested finite product of `gaussianReal 0 1` laws on all n² real entries. The second Gaussian parameter is variance. An explicit measurable-space instance is exactly the ordinary Pi measurable space on those entries, not a discrete approximation. A required exported contract proves probability normalization, joint independence of all entries and every standard-normal marginal.

## Actual GEPP and probability semantics

`states A π` recursively applies an actual row swap and Schur complement for the entire finite pivot schedule π. The eliminated rows and columns are padded with zero, so state k contains exactly the active trailing matrix. There are no column swaps. `IsLegal` includes every row of largest absolute magnitude in the active first column, and requires a nonzero pivot at every stage. The numerator of `pathGrowth` is a genuine finite maximum over every entry in every state 0,...,n−1; its denominator is the genuine maximum absolute input entry. Thus it includes the input and final scalar state.

`growthValues` ranges over all legal schedules. `ppGrowth` is its actual real sSup. For every nonsingular positive-dimensional matrix, `growth_semantics` must prove positive denominator, nonempty finite bounded values, an attained greatest value, and equivalence between a tail of this supremum and existence of a legal path exceeding the threshold. It also requires a unique first-available schedule as an auxiliary deterministic convention. That convention does not restrict the main all-tie supremum.

The tail event excludes singular perturbed matrices. This is an explicit extension convention, supported by a separate `gaussian_generic` obligation proving Gaussian almost-sure nonsingularity and uniqueness of the entire legal pivot schedule for every deterministic center and every positive noise level. Therefore the all-tie supremum agrees almost surely with the original unique GEPP path, and the singular-input convention cannot change the probability. `measurable_tail` must prove actual Borel measurability of each event. No unjustified measurability or null-set assumption is supplied to the conjecture.

## Universal deterministic and numerical obligations

For every n≥2, W_n has final column one, diagonal one, strict-lower entries −1/2 and remaining entries zero. The exact no-swap active state k has the original triangular pattern and final column (3/2)^k, padded by zero outside the active indices. The exported theorem requires that these are the actual recursive states, all legal schedules are the no-swap schedule, W_n is nonsingular and its true growth equals c_n=(3/2)^(n−1).

Use B_n=2^(n+2), δ_n=1/2^(n²+n+1), and K_n=n²(n²+n+5). Prove B_n^(n−1)δ_n=1/8 and K_n≤3n⁴. The robustness theorem quantifies over **every real matrix in the full closed n²-dimensional entrywise box** |A_ij−W_ij|≤δ_n. It concludes nonsingularity, legality and uniqueness of the no-swap schedule, strict active pivot dominance, the full recursive error bound e_k≤B_n^kδ_n for k<n, and actual growth greater than c_n/2.

The intended proof keeps the induction scalar: e≤1/8 gives pivot≥7/8 and competitors≤5/8; the multiplier satisfies |q|≤5/7 and |q+1/2|≤2e. The actual difference of Schur updates then yields e_next≤(2+2^(n+1))e≤B_ne. No exponential enumeration of the box vertices is needed. The final input maximum≤9/8 and final pivot≥c_n−1/8 imply growth>c_n/2. These are proof obligations, not established facts of this draft.

On the actual Gaussian scalar interval [−2,2], prove gaussianPDFReal(0,1,x)>1/32. A possible LeanCert route is a material point bound exp(−2)/sqrt(2π)>1/32 plus exact monotonicity on the full interval, avoiding interval subdivision or sampled-grid claims. The identity-centered noise box is the inverse image of the whole input box under G↦I+G. Its actual probability must be at least (δ_n/16)^(n²)=2^(−K_n), by genuine independence and the interval density bound. The deterministic robustness theorem then gives the actual Gaussian tail bound for I+G. The extra source case center=0 is not needed to refute the full universal claim and is not advertised.

For arbitrary positive real c₁,c₂, set x_n=c_n/(2 n^c₁). Prove eventually n≥2, x_n≥1 and c₂x_n>K_n. Then the identity center has actual Euclidean norm one, σ=1 is admissible, and the lower probability exceeds 2^(−c₂x_n) at the original threshold x_n n^c₁. Every proposed pair is violated, yielding the complete negation.

## Reuse, feasibility and gates

The existing IE-05 formalization supplies a reviewed example of genuine row-swap/padded-Schur semantics and finite entry maxima; credit remains with its formalization author Sidney Holden and Codex assistance. No theorem from that NLA project is imported. Pinned Mathlib supplies real Gaussian density/probability measures, finite Pi measures and independence, matrix determinants, the Euclidean operator norm, and real exponential/rpow asymptotics. LeanCert is pinned for eventual material numerical certification and actual export kernel trust audits.

Concrete new infrastructure is still substantial: general legal GEPP path existence and elimination determinant/invertibility; Borel measurability of recursively chosen finite schedules and their maximum; almost-sure nonsingularity and absence of pivot ties for arbitrary Gaussian-smoothed centers; a generic full-box perturbation induction; conversion of product interval measures to the retained event; and the all-real-exponent asymptotic comparison. No existing library theorem has been identified that packages all these bridges. The draft explicitly retains those obligations rather than dropping them.

`verification/exact-precheck.py` reconstructs exact rational states and scalar identities for n=2,...,12; its output is diagnostic only. It does not verify the universal box, probability or asymptotic conclusions. There are twelve deliberate Challenge placeholders and no Solution/proof implementation. Successful statement elaboration, once recorded, establishes only well-formedness. Two independent statement approvals, full proof development, two final reviews, all-export kernel/axiom inspection and an actual isolated Linux Comparator run remain required before a completion claim.
